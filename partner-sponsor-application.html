About NTE27

NTE27 returns to The Wing at Silverstone on Monday 1 March 2027, following an event that welcomed more than 1,200 members of the Armed Forces community, over 200 exhibiting organisations and more than 100 Troops' Track Day supercars. Delivered by Mission Community and Mission Motorsport, the National Transition Event is the largest annual gathering of the UK Armed Forces community. Incorporating the Troops' Track Day, it brings together Service Leavers, Veterans, Reservists, spouses and partners, family members and Cadets with Armed Forces-friendly companies, government agencies and charities to exchange insight, lived experience and career opportunities. Unlike a traditional Jobs Fair, NTE27 is designed for meaningful networking, collaboration and lasting relationships.
