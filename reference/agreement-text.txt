This letter and attached Schedule sets out the terms and arrangements of sponsorship, in particular, key definitions and details (Event, Venue, Sponsorship Fee, Promotional Materials, Sponsor Branding, Promotion, Event Services, Intellectual Property Rights).
The Sponsor will provide the Organiser with financial sponsorship for the National Transition Event and the Organiser will in return appoint the Sponsor as non-exclusive sponsor and will promote the Sponsor’s name and business at the Event.
The Organiser’s responsibilities
The Organiser undertakes to:
host, organise, and run the National Transition Event and provide the Venue;
provide the Sponsor with the Promotion and the Event Services (at and in relation to the Event) as detailed in the Schedule;
prepare and provide the Promotional Materials, and use them as detailed in the Schedule;
neither make any public or other statement about the Sponsor or its business, products or services that contains negative, derogatory, or misleading content, nor use any Sponsor Branding nor any other branding nor any other item relating to the Sponsor's business without the Sponsor's prior approval (whether or not it relates to the Event), except that the Organiser may make factual statements about the Sponsor's sponsorship of the Event for legitimate promotional, marketing, or coordination purposes and may use the Sponsor Branding as permitted by Clause 2.3 below and as detailed in the Schedule;
not do anything at or in relation to the Event likely to harm the Sponsor’s business reputation or goodwill attaching to its business or Sponsor Branding; and
ensure that it complies with all legal requirements and that it has all necessary authorities, licences, and consents to run the Event, to meet its obligations under this Agreement and for the parties to implement this Agreement generally.
The Sponsor’s responsibilities

The Sponsor undertakes:
to pay the Organiser the Sponsorship Fee as set out in the Schedule;
to provide the Organiser in good time with suitable electronic copies of the Sponsor Branding to use in the Promotional Materials;
to permit the Organiser to use the Sponsor Branding as necessary to enable the Organiser to provide the Sponsor Promotion and Event Services to host, organise, and run the Event, but does not grant the Organiser any other right or licence under the Sponsor’s Intellectual Property Rights;
that the Sponsor Branding does not and will not infringe any Intellectual Property Rights of any third party, and the Sponsor will indemnify and hold harmless the Organiser against any claims, damages, costs, and expenses (including reasonable legal fees) arising from any third-party infringement claims related to the Organiser's use of the Sponsor Branding in accordance with this Agreement; and
to advertise or make any public statements about the Sponsor’s sponsorship of the Event only if such statements do not contain any negative comment about the Organiser or the Event.
Circumstances beyond the reasonable control of either party
If the Organiser or the Sponsor (“party”) fails to perform or delays in performing any obligation due to circumstance beyond that party’s reasonable control (“Force Majeure”), that party shall not be considered to be in breach of or liable for that failure or delay.

Termination
For force majeure
Where there is Force Majeure and as a result it reasonably appears to either party at any time that the Event cannot commence, or cannot continue until its conclusion as or when contemplated by this Agreement, either party may promptly at that time terminate this Agreement by notice to the other party.
If, at the time of serving that notice, any two or more of the promotional activities specified in clause 4.1.2.2 below have not been completed:
either  (a) the Event has not commenced; or
(b) the Event has commenced, but fewer than two of the promotional activities specified in clause 4.1.2.2 below have been completed - none of the Sponsorship Fee shall be payable, and if any has been paid it shall be promptly returned to the Sponsor; or
the Event has commenced and any two or more of the following promotional activities have been completed: (i) social media posts featuring the Sponsor's branding have been published, (ii) the Sponsor's logo has been displayed on the event website for at least 7 days, (iii) promotional materials containing the Sponsor's branding have been distributed, or (iv) the Event has run for at least 25% of its scheduled duration, then 50% of the Sponsorship Fee shall be and remain payable, and any sum previously paid over and above 50% shall be promptly returned to the Sponsor.

For no good reason
If the Organiser cancels the Event at a time when there is no Force Majeure and there is no breach of this Agreement by the Sponsor, the Organiser may do so by notice to the Sponsor. In such circumstances: (a) if cancellation occurs more than 60 days before the Event, the Organiser may retain up to 25% of the Sponsorship Fee to cover reasonable costs incurred, with the remainder promptly returned to the Sponsor; (b) if cancellation occurs 60 days or fewer before the Event, the Organiser may retain up to 50% of the Sponsorship Fee to cover reasonable costs incurred, with the remainder promptly returned to the Sponsor.

For breach
Either party may terminate this Agreement by giving notice to the other if the other commits any breach of a material obligation under this Agreement (including failure to pay any sum due) and fails to remedy it within 30 days after being given notice containing details of the breach and requiring it to be remedied. Upon such termination, if the party in breach is:
4.3.1the Organiser, it may not retain or receive any Sponsorship Fee, and it must promptly return any of it previously paid; or
4.3.2    the Sponsor, the Organiser will be entitled to receive and retain a proportion of the Sponsorship Fee corresponding to the promotional activities and Event Services already provided or committed to at the time of breach, with any excess amount to be promptly returned to the Sponsor.

Confidentiality
Except as the parties may otherwise agree in relation to any particular information, each party undertakes to the other to keep confidential all confidential information of the other, to use it only if and to the extent necessary to carry out this Agreement,and not disclose any such confidential information to any third party. This undertaking shall not apply to any information which: (a) is in the public domain otherwise than due to a breach of this undertaking; (b) is required to be disclosed by law, regulation, or court order; (c) is necessary for event promotion, marketing, or coordination activities contemplated by this Agreement; (d) is disclosed to professional advisers, auditors, or other service providers under appropriate confidentiality obligations; or (e) relates to the existence and general terms of this sponsorship arrangement for legitimate business purposes including coordination with other event participants.
